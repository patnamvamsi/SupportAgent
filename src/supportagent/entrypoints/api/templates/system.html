How to read this
  1. Each shape is a part of the system: spheres are services, diamonds are jobs, cylinders hold data, rings are topics.
  2. Lines show what talks to what. A dashed line is a fact SupportAgent proposed and nobody has confirmed yet.
  3. Select a part to fly to it. Its connections unfold, and this panel becomes its file.
  4. A red ring means the part has an open case.
  5. A red marker above a part is an open case's alert; the violet line is where the agent took it.
  6. Drag to turn the model, scroll to zoom, Esc to step back out.
